Project Notes & Rationale

Hunch (before AI)

"Independent surf schools and outdoor activity providers in Ireland would pay for automated weather-cancellation software because manual phone rescheduling wastes hours of staff time and loses revenue from unrecovered bookings."

Task 1: Thinking & Strategy

Chosen Niche:

Independent surf and kite-surfing schools along the Wild Atlantic Way.

  • Buyer: Surf school owner.
  • User: Head instructor or desk manager.
  • Pain (Invented quote): "When a big storm hits, I spend three hours texting forty people off a paper diary, half don't reply, and we lose hundreds in deposits."

Technology & Competitors:

Product Type: Automated booking management and weather-cancellation rescheduling portal.
Competitors: Checkfront (checked Oct 19, 2026) and Bookwhen (checked Oct 19, 2026).

Business Model (Assumption):

€99 set-up fee plus €49 per month, reasoned as an affordable SaaS model for seasonal outdoor businesses that saves multiple hours of administration per weather cancellation.

Lean PRD:

  1. Hypothesis: Automated session cancellation rebooking recovers 80% of weather-cancelled slots within 48 hours.
  2. Audience: Independent outdoor activity providers in Ireland.
  3. Call to Action: Request early access / join waiting list.
  4. Success Metric: Conversion rate of visitors clicking "Try Demo" and submitting the waitlist form.
  5. Out of Scope: Payment processing gateways, multi-currency support, and automated SMS carrier integration.

Alpha Test Log

What was tried: Tested the mobile layout on an iPhone, checking the "Today" view cancellation flow and the 48-hour red alert timer on the "Rebook" tab.

What broke / confused: Initially, the "Cancel Session" button was too small to tap easily with wet/cold hands, and the text wrapping on mobile screens looked cramped.

What was changed: Updated the CSS to make touch targets full-width with large padding for one-handed use, and increased font sizes for outdoor readability.
